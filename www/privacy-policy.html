Privacy Policy

Heartland Weather Desk

Effective: October 8, 2026

Heartland Weather Desk ("the app," "we," "us") is a weather, sports, and outdoors app for the Midwest. We built it to be useful without being nosy. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information. In plain terms: we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, we do not run analytics or trackers, and we do not require an account.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, names, emails, or passwords.
  • No advertising identifiers; no ads.
  • No analytics, behavioral tracking, or third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information with data brokers — ever.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or background location.

Push alerts (optional)

If — and only if — you turn on severe-weather push notifications, the app sends our alert service your device's push token, the platform (iOS/Android), and the coordinates/labels of the locations you want watched (up to ten). A scheduled job checks the National Weather Service for those points and sends you a notification when there's a severe or extreme alert. We store the token, those locations, and the IDs of alerts already sent (so you aren't notified twice). You can stop this at any time by disabling notifications; turning them off removes your device from the watch list, and tokens that are no longer valid are deleted automatically.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Location: you can deny location permission and search by city/ZIP instead; the app still works.
  • Push alerts: optional, off until you enable them, and removable by turning notifications off.
  • Local data: clearing the app's data / your browser storage erases saved places and settings.
